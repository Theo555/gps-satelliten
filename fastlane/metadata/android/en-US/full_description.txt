GPS Satelliten is a GNSS tool for Android: satellite status, signal
quality, position and raw data – plus offline navigation with waypoints,
tracks and geocaches.

The screens
* Radar: all visible satellites on a sky view, with a bearing to the
  active target
* Signals: signal strength per satellite, filterable by constellation
  and frequency band
* List: all satellites with elevation, azimuth and status, sortable
* Position: coordinates in decimal, DMS and DDM, altitude, speed and DOP
  values
* Navigation: waypoints, tracks and geocaches with GPX or JSON import
  and export
* Measurements: raw GNSS data with pseudorange, Doppler, carrier phase
  and clock drift – on devices that provide these values

Navigation and geocaching
* GPX import based on a real XML parser, including cache groups, hint,
  difficulty and terrain rating
* Record tracks, follow them and reverse their direction
* Bearing, remaining distance and an arrival alert while following a
  target
* Hint shown in plain text or as ROT13
* Map view is handed over to an installed map app
* Simulation mode to try things out without GPS reception

Offline and without data collection
* No internet permission – the app cannot send any data
* No accounts, no ads, no usage analytics
* Location, waypoints and tracks stay on the device
* Files are only written when you export them yourself

Honest data
Whatever the hardware does not deliver is shown as a dash. The app does
not invent values.

The app is free, ad-free and released under the MIT licence. It runs on
Android 7.0 and newer.
