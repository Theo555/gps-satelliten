1.3.8
* The Measure tab now stays available on devices where raw-data
  registration fails briefly at startup (e.g. Note 9 on Android 10) –
  registration is retried several times
* Measurement values consistently use a decimal point (previously a row
  could mix point and comma)
* The app stays in portrait – the landscape layout was not readable
* If the receiver sends no raw data although satellites are visible,
  the Measure tab explains this and names both remedies (turn location
  off for a minute, or close the app and wait ~90 s); the display
  recovers on its own afterwards
