1.3.3
* GPX import now uses a real XML parser instead of regular expressions
  and copes with CDATA, namespaces and unknown elements
* The import reports how many caches and waypoints were taken over and
  which entries were skipped
* Hint in the active target: plain text by default, switchable to ROT13
* Tracks can be followed in reverse direction
* Measurements: clock drift and leap second are shown as unknown instead
  of a number, pseudorange is calculated again
