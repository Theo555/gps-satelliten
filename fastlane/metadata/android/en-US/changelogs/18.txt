1.3.13
* Raw data export: BeiDou and GLONASS now provide usable pseudoranges - these
  systems had the wrong time base so far. A new column states the reason when
  a pseudorange is missing
* Carrier phase is resolved to 0.1 mm instead of 0.1 m (a thousand times finer)
* Measurement view: the filter now names the satellite systems that are
  actually received instead of the L1/L5 bands
* Dialogs: the confirm button is filled and therefore distinguishable from
  cancel
* Satellite list: the sort button label is no longer truncated
* New Compose version; Android 7 is still supported
