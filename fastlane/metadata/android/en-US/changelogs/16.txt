1.3.11
* New: compass check in the radar tab. A rotation test against the gyroscope
  shows whether the compass readings are credible at this place - with
  progress, result and hints (case, magnetic clasp, figure-eight motion).
  Nothing is corrected
* A disturbing environment is reported as such (metal, wiring, nearby
  devices) instead of being blamed on the device
* The compass note now names the reason and the measured value, e.g.
  "field 81 µT (too strong)"
* Fixed: on some devices the compass note was permanently on
