1.3.10
* Navigation tab reorganized: the active target or track stays on top, below
  it a bar with the three common actions and four collapsible sections
  (caches, other waypoints, tracks, files) - only one is open at a time
* Recording: "Stop" now offers save, discard or keep recording. When
  collapsed, the point count and a stop button stay visible
* Tracks are stored as individual files - saving stays fast with many or
  long tracks, and a defect affects only one track (existing tracks are
  migrated on first start)
* Bearing: while moving, the arrow follows the GPS course and the compass is
  used when standing; the track arrow stays on the line. A note appears when
  the compass is unreliable
* Raw data: pseudorange now uses exact integer arithmetic (previously up to
  75 m off), numbers consistently use a dot as decimal separator
* Without a GPS fix the last values stay visible but are greyed out
* Import reports skipped entries in a dialog instead of a toast
