1.3.12
* Fixed: following long tracks froze the display, because the remaining
  distance was recomputed from every point on each position update
* While following a track, the number next to the arrow shows the distance
  left in the current direction instead of the distance to the next point
* Arrival alert: the radius follows the reported accuracy - the more precise
  the fix, the closer you have to get. It triggers only after two readings in
  a row, re-arms after a restart and also works at the end of a track
* Compass: local magnetic declination is corrected, so heading and GPS course
  agree; the warning is now also shown on the nav page
* Radar: the hint line keeps its height, nothing jumps anymore
* Location and sensors follow the app lifecycle (less drain in background)
* JSON export keeps the cache grouping
